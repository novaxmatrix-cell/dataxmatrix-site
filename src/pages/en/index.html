About us

Why DataX?

Most school software is designed for an ideal school: full days, every teacher entering data on time, a connection that never drops. Real schools don’t work like that. The timetable can change mid-term, one person may carry absences and covers for the whole school, and the official paper is needed on the letterhead now.

So we build on one principle: one committed person is enough to run the school from one computer, without waiting on anyone.

One person is enough

The principal, deputy or secretary enters, prints and follows up alone; nothing waits on every teacher entering their own data.

Works offline

The program and its data live on the school’s computer. The connection drops, the work goes on.

Official and actual, side by side

What the Ministry and directorate approve stays as it is, and next to it sits what the school actually lives.

Microsoft Store only

Installs and updates come from the Store, so each new version arrives on its own.

Our approach

How we work

  1. We listen to the field
