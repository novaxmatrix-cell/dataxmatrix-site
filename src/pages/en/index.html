Features

What Iris does for your school

Import instead of typing

The Ministry system file as is, even if its columns move; all section files at once with one preview; staff and attendance from ready Excel templates.

Covers

Leave and cover lessons, with the best cover suggested first: a teacher of the same subject, or one who teaches the section, with the load shared fairly. The daily covers list is ready to print.

Duty rota

Several people per spot, the days you choose, a fair fill, and a sheet ready to print.

Emergency plan

Set the in-school days and the distance-learning days, run an actual timetable beside the official one, and remote days count as present for everyone.

Parents’ phones

One number per parent reaches all their children, and sections missing numbers are flagged.

On your letterhead

The daily absence report, the covers list, the duty sheet and the student statement: on your school’s letterhead, in Arabic and English.

Backup

An automatic copy to a USB drive you can restore when needed, plus OneDrive if you want it.

Also in Iris

Tuition, parent statements, the overdue list and the daily cash book; teaching and exams; correspondence; moving students between sections with a history.

Your school’s data

Safe, at your school

Daily work needs no internet. The program and its data stay on the school’s computer.

Keeps working when the connection drops

All daily work happens on the school’s computer, even when the connection drops. When online, you’ll see an alert about new updates.

Encrypted

The school’s data is stored encrypted on its own computer.

Stays at the school

Student and staff data is never uploaded to any external server, except the OneDrive backup if the school turns it on.

Automatic backup

An automatic backup to a USB drive that you can restore when needed, and optional backup to OneDrive.
