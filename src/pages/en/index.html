What Iris does

Everything the principal and secretary handle, in one place

Built around the forms and procedures of Palestinian schools.

Students and attendance

  • Students

    Import the Ministry system file as is, assign sections, keep parents and their phone numbers, and move students between sections with a history.

  • Attendance

    Take attendance for each section, print the daily absence report, and handle warnings and referrals.

Staff and teaching

  • Leave and substitutes

    Share substitute lessons fairly and print the daily substitutes list.

  • Teaching and exams

    The study plan, lesson allocation, exam rooms and invigilation.

Finance and office

  • Tuition and finance

    Tuition and installments, sibling discount, uniform, book and transport fees. Account statements for parents, an overdue payments list, a daily cash book, and receipts that are voided with a written reason instead of deleted.

  • Correspondence

    Incoming and outgoing letters, reminders and notes.

Your school’s data

Safe, at your school

Works without internet and uses no cloud servers. The program and its data stay on the school’s computer.

Works offline

All daily work happens on the school’s computer, even when the connection drops. When online, you’ll see an alert about new updates.

Encrypted

The school’s data is stored encrypted on its own computer.

Stays at the school

Student and staff data is never uploaded to any external server, except the OneDrive backup if the school turns it on.

Automatic backup

An automatic backup to a USB drive that you can restore when needed, and optional backup to OneDrive.
