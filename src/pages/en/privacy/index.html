Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This page explains how DataX Matrix’s apps handle data: Iris (school management) and Iris Exam (worksheets and tests).

Iris

Your school’s data is stored encrypted on the school’s own computer.

When you request the free trial, we receive the contact details you type in the form, use them only to review the request, and do not share them with anyone.

Questions: WhatsApp

Iris Exam

  • Iris Exam stores only on the teacher’s computer: the teacher’s name, WhatsApp number, the sheets they make, and the licence.
  • Iris Exam sends no data anywhere, not to us and not to any third party, and works without internet.
  • Activation is a code exchange: the teacher chooses to send us the request code on WhatsApp, and we send back an activation code. What you write to us on WhatsApp is used only for activation and support.

This website

dataxmatrix.com uses no cookies and no tracking or analytics tools. Your browser only stores your dark mode and language choices.

The site links to Iris’s page on the Microsoft Store and to our YouTube channel, and the user guide videos are hosted on YouTube: a video loads from YouTube only after you press play; until then the page shows only a thumbnail stored on this site. Once you open those links or play a video, Microsoft’s or Google’s privacy terms apply.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes in how Iris or Iris Exam handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date at the top.
