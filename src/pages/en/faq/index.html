Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions. Anything else: WhatsApp.

Does Iris need internet?

Iris needs the internet when you request the trial, and then only from time to time. Daily work happens on the school’s computer; the internet is also used for updates from the Microsoft Store and, if the school turns it on, the OneDrive backup.

Where is the school’s data stored?

Only on the school’s computer, with an automatic backup to a USB drive.

We changed computers. Do we need a new activation?

Yes. Contact us on WhatsApp, and we activate your subscription on the new computer. Activation & pricing
